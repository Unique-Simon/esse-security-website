ABOUT ESSE

ESSE Security Services provides professional security personnel, recruitment, training, and security-related support to organisations across North Central Nigeria.

We deliver practical security solutions tailored to the needs of businesses, institutions, and other establishments. Our services include physical security personnel, CCTV operation, intelligence reporting, and health and safety training.

We believe effective security requires trained people, responsible conduct, awareness of potential risks, and a clear understanding of each client's operational needs.
